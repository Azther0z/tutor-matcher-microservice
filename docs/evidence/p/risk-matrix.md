# C-06 risk matrix draft (P: money, access, notification and delivery)

Draft for Saint to assemble with Ideal's search risks, Santa's lifecycle risks and Saint's access/deployment risks. Likelihood and impact are 1 (low) to 5 (high), judged for the course-demo environment. "Evidence" cites only checks that actually ran; anything unrun is marked.

| ID | Risk | L | I | Mitigation | Evidence | Remaining risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | **Money inconsistency**: a retry, race or crash double-charges, double-refunds, double-clears or over-refunds | 3 | 5 | One financial-record row lock for charge, close, refund, completion, dispute and clearing; immutable command hash per operation identity; unique ledger `(type, operation_id)`; single applied payment per booking via a partial unique index; refund cap check and constraint; deterministic IDs for remedy and earning | 86 Wallet tests on PostgreSQL incl. 10 concurrent charge-versus-close and 12 dispute-versus-clearing runs; removing the lock failed 2 tests; cap and boundary fixtures from `cases.json` pass | **Medium.** Not yet exercised with the real Booking, restored backups, or crash-injection mid-transaction. Booking's use of the answers (confirm vs refund after a lost response) is Santa's half |
| R-02 | **Unknown provider/transport outcome treated as failure**, releasing funds or a hold too early | 3 | 5 | UNKNOWN keeps payout reservations and pending operations; a gRPC deadline never releases money; `GetLessonCharge` NOT_FOUND is documented as non-proof, only `ResolveOrCloseLessonCharge` writes a CLOSED tombstone | UNKNOWN payout/top-up tests; tombstone-then-late-charge test over gRPC | **Low-medium.** Depends on Booking calling resolve-or-close before releasing slots |
| R-03 | **Access violation**: a user reads or changes another's wallet, payout accounts, disputes, receipts or preferences, or calls demo/admin routes | 2 | 5 | Backend capability from the signed actor assertion; every query scoped by `actor.userId`; 404 for non-owned resources; payout numbers never stored; demo routes need ADMIN + `DEMO_MODE` + synthetic resource; unsigned `X-User-Id` ignored | HTTP tests for 401/403/404 across both services, expired assertion, cross-user payout account and top-up access; event bodies checked for bank-number material | **Medium.** Real Gateway signing, a banned account holding an unexpired JWT, and Auth being down are untested because Gateway/Auth are not integrated |
| R-04 | **Broker or dependency failure** loses or duplicates notifications, or blocks money | 3 | 3 | Outbox written with the decision; leased, fenced publisher with jittered backoff; `mandatory` routing treats unroutable as retryable; Notification acknowledges after one transaction containing event, audience work and jobs; unique effect per event/recipient/channel; peer outages leave jobs pending | Outbox, acceptance, lease-takeover and consumer-decision tests against fakes and real MongoDB; Wallet booted with RabbitMQ unreachable | **High until run.** No real RabbitMQ, Traefik, Swarm or restart/restore test was executed (see README) |
| R-05 | **Instructor does not accept simulated providers** (open item in requirements) | 3 | 4 | Everything is visibly labelled a demonstration; no payable QR; ADMIN-only outcome controls; success/failure/unknown fully modelled so a real adapter can replace the simulator behind the same operation identities | Representation text and `demo: true` on every resource and receipt | **Medium.** A scope question only the instructor can settle; keep it on the risk slide and ask before 10 November |
| R-06 | **Clearing deadline wrong** (cleared early, or delayed by late completion delivery) | 2 | 4 | Deadline fixed at the original `tutorConfirmedAt` plus 24 h; conflicting replacement rejected; sweep derives due work from the database | Tests: 24 h minus 1 ms does not clear; completion delivered 24 h late keeps the deadline; dispute held blocks clearing; rejection keeps the deadline | **Low.** Injected time proves the logic; the live demo must label fixtures as historical, as the handoff requires |
| R-07 | **Contract drift between owners** (error codes, `_field` decoder markers, balance meaning) | 4 | 3 | [contract-findings.md](contract-findings.md) lists each mismatch and its owner | Found by running real gRPC round trips | **Medium.** Open until Saint/Santa review and the first integration |
| R-08 | **Version skew**: tests ran on PostgreSQL 18 and MongoDB 8, deployment pins 16 and 7; standalone Mongo cannot take transactions | 2 | 4 | Plain SQL and standard Mongo transaction API; replica-set requirement documented | None beyond the local run | **Medium.** Re-run both suites against the pinned images |

Not rated here: the load-test and C4 deliverables (Ideal, Saint).
